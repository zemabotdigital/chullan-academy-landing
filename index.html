Before you book

Questions academies ask.

What happens on the first call?
About an hour, on a phone call or Google Meet — whichever you prefer. We ask about your courses, batch sizes and what your students struggle with, then take you through the eight-product kit and how academies use it. If it looks like a fit, we schedule the product demo before the call ends.
What happens after the call?
We schedule the product demo at your academy — hands-on with all eight products and live shade matching with your trainers. Your sample kit is sent after the intro call, one per academy.
Will you have shades for all my students?
Eight foundation shades, fair neutral to deep warm, across two formulations — Professional Base and the more luminous Pro Glow. Every shade is engineered for Indian undertones rather than adapted from a global range. The concealer comes in three warm shades and the anti-shine powder in four.
Will it hold up in our working conditions?
The foundation range is stability tested at 40°C and conforms to IS 14318:1996. The moisturiser and sunscreen are dermatologically tested under IS 4011:2018 guidelines and classified Non-Irritant. Shelf life is three years.
What about kit size and pricing?
Kit size and pilot terms are set around your batch size and course length, so there's no one-size price list. We confirm everything on the intro call. No commitment.
Where are the products made?
Formulated in the USA and manufactured in India. Paraben free and mineral oil free across all eight products, with an alcohol-free setting spray.
